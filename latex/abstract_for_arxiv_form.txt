Cloud-local inference sends a de-identified artifact after local gate approval. We audit one runtime-S chain on frozen synthetic benchmarks, scoped to this implementation. Three core findings: (1) missed extraction causes false release — the chain releases 57.5% of a shared-vocab holdout, yet 78.3% of released instances (Wilson [64.4,87.7]) retain gold-annotated literals, banking 20/20 with residue; (2) the utility gate rewards unknown task coverage — with no task attribute extracted, utility falls back to unmarked-context retention, so worse extraction makes release easier (65% of releases pass through this harmful fallback, all with residue); a coverage gate leaves 5/80 releases with 0 observed false releases (Wilson upper bound 43.4%) — too few to validate safety; (3) the closed loop adds no benefit over one-shot deterministic generalization — paired release-rate and gold-residue differences are zero on three holdouts, local utility is lower on all three, and downstream reference-answer preservation shows no detectable mean difference. A disjoint-vocabulary stress test and dose-response series show recall collapsing with overlap; fixing clear defects removes observed false release on shared vocabulary (0/46) but not under shift. A diagnostic cloud neural extractor keeps recall 0.965 under full displacement; full-displacement residue is undeclared room_no. Marriage_years and work_years were also omitted but caused no released residue. On a new holdout, each added type extracts 10/10 with no released residue, while one within-taxonomy miss persists (1/32); taxonomy-gap zero is definitional, not safety evidence. Gold-literal residue is an audit proxy, not demonstrated privacy harm; city/county causation remains unresolved. We contribute a reproducible audit framework with conditional false-release metrics and public raw outputs, not certified safety.
