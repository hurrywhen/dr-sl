# Declarations and Disclosure Statements — Journal Submission

Manuscript: **Auditing Local Release Gates for Cloud-Local LLM Inference: Failure Modes of a Runtime-Sensitive-Set De-identification Chain**
Authors: Wen Hu, Ya Yu, Xutong Wang

> **Positioning note (round 6).** This manuscript is a failure-analysis and reproducible-audit study of a runtime-S release chain, not a claim of a superior automatic sanitizer. The closed loop is an audited object; the title no longer uses "Reversibility-Verified" (no rehydration/reversibility experiment is run in the deterministic track). The audited artifact retains the legacy repository name DR-SL; self-learning is exploratory and excluded from the confirmatory release path. **All conclusions are scoped to the audited implementation and the author-constructed synthetic tests; they do not estimate failure rates of other local gates or of real deployments.** Round 3 corrected the tied-rank Spearman error and reran the contaminated downstream experiment (invalid outputs archived). Round 4 corrected two paired-difference direction errors, reported both judges in full, scoped zero-event claims to their sample sizes, renamed the fallback basis to unmarked-context retention, and added the diagnostic cloud-assisted neural-extractor contrast. Round 5 corrects that contrast's interpretation: the frozen taxonomy omitted three gold types (automatic coverage test added); residual false release is decomposed into taxonomy-coverage gaps (all of holdout-1/drift-p0) vs within-declared-taxonomy neural misses (holdout-3); the blanket "lower bound" claim is replaced by direction-specific scoping (exact-span recall conservative under the prompt/taxonomy; FR conditional, no bound direction); income misses are partial-span extraction reported with three coverage layers. Pre-registered credibility experiments add sampling-stability repeats (identical release/FR instance sets across three runs on a 60-instance subset), a second model family (glm-5.2; different miss identity, same inheritance), and a complete-taxonomy holdout-4 contrast. Round 6 corrects the three GLM zero-charge cases: the extended fee span was proposed and scrubbed, while the short form repeated in the clarification remains; symmetric proposal/relation tests replace the erroneous "never proposed" label. Complete-taxonomy FR_gap=0 is definitional, not safety evidence; each added h4 type is exactly extracted 10/10 with zero released residue. The repeated county literal has unresolved value/template and city/county/address explanations. Gold-literal residue is an audit proxy, not demonstrated privacy harm; excluding zero-charge residues changes only GLM-subset FR (5/25→2/25). All neural conditions remain cloud-assisted and diagnostic only (extraction itself discloses X); none is a deployable local gate, and the local neural service remains unavailable.

## 5. Ethics statement

- **Human studies.** Two studies: (1) semantic-utility anchor — two raters independently scored 160 blinded development rows (40 instances × 4 operating points; blind key withheld until scoring finished); (2) annotation audit — two annotators independently listed sensitive items and judged combination re-identification risk on 50 instances. The only participants were co-authors Y. Yu and X. Wang (expert raters, independent of the pipeline implementation), participating voluntarily with full knowledge of their co-authorship; they were blinded to variant identities and automatic scores during scoring. No third-party subjects, no crowdsourced labor, no personal or clinical data; all evaluated text is fully synthetic. A blinded holdout utility scoring packet is prepared but **not yet collected**; no synthetic "human" scores were invented, and holdout utility claims rest on external judges only. Under [INSTITUTION] policy such non-subject evaluation of synthetic material is exempt from IRB review; [confirm exemption wording/number before submission]. Preregistered criteria and outcomes (including the failed original combination-risk κ and the post hoc construct-aligned analysis) are reported in full.
- **Privacy of local assets.** The manuscript itself analyzes and mitigates the sensitivity of system-internal artifacts (experience store, residual reports, audit logs); no such artifact containing sensitive values is published.
- **Dual-use.** The attack/verification methodology is defensive (release gating); experiments use only synthetic data and provider APIs under their terms.
