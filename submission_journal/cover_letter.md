# Cover Letter — Journal Submission (Revised, Round 6)

Dear Editor,

We submit the revised manuscript **Auditing Local Release Gates for Cloud-Local LLM Inference: Failure Modes of a Runtime-Sensitive-Set De-identification Chain**.

This round addresses the sixth review's two required factual/interpretation corrections and its construct-validity qualifications. The Route-A positioning is unchanged: this is an audit case study of one runtime-S implementation on author-constructed synthetic benchmarks, not a claim of a superior sanitizer or a deployable neural gate. The three core findings, all release decisions and all primary FR numerators/denominators are unchanged. **No new model calls or datasets were introduced; every new number is recomputed from archived outputs, and the round-4/round-5 raw neural archives remain byte-intact.**

**1. Zero-charge mechanism corrected.** The previous phrase “three zero-valued charges never proposed” was wrong. GLM's raw outputs contain `charge = 0元手续费`; that extended span was localized and scrubbed, while the short `0元` form repeated in the appended clarification remained. These are therefore **gold-substring-of-proposal / span-variant repeated-mention residues**, not complete extraction omissions. `fr_decomposition` now handles five symmetric relations: exact proposal, proposal substring of gold, gold substring of proposal, related-but-noncontaining proposal, and never proposed. Regression tests cover all five relations, the archived three GLM cases, and a synthetic long/short repeated mention in which scrubbing the extended span leaves the short gold literal. This correction changes mechanism interpretation, not FR values.

**2. Holdout-4 evidence stated without a definitional success claim.** Under the complete 43-type taxonomy, `FR_taxonomy_gap = 0/32` is a **definitional identity**, because every h4 gold type is declared by construction. It is no longer presented as independent extraction or safety improvement. The substantive evidence is direct: room number, marriage duration and work tenure each achieve **10/10 exact type-and-value extraction with zero released residue**. City values are **59/60 exact by value** under the paper's existing recall convention and **54/60 with a city label** (five are labelled address). Total FR remains **1/32 [0.6, 15.7]**, so the gate still inherits a within-taxonomy residue after taxonomy completion.

**3. Persistent city residue is not over-explained.** The repeated county literal is now described as a reproducible omission with two unresolved explanations: value/template-specific omission and a city/county/administrative-region versus address type boundary. We removed the established “value-specific blind spot” wording. A minimal city/county and broader-location controlled comparison is identified as future work rather than run as another expansion of the experiment.

**4. Gold-literal construct and zero-charge sensitivity are explicit.** `FR_gold` is a gold-annotated task/sensitive **literal-residue audit proxy**, not demonstrated privacy damage or re-identification. The paper distinguishes explicit identifiers, quasi-identifiers, and task-critical values whose stand-alone sensitivity depends on context, combinations, auxiliary knowledge and linkability. `0元` is conservatively annotated under the personal-financial and banking-task rules; that does not assert that a zero fee alone is private or identifying. We also disclose that whole-thousand quantization in the frozen small-fee generator produces zero values; frozen data are not retroactively altered. In the additional exclusion sensitivity, only GLM-subset FR changes (**5/25 → 2/25**, both taxonomy gaps); every main deterministic and other neural FR count is unchanged. The primary metric remains unmodified.

**Organization and verification.** The main neural subsection is rewritten in place, with detailed per-instance evidence in `neural_round6_analysis.json` rather than new main-text tables. Page targets remain main 19 pp., journal 11 pp. and conference 4 pp., with zero overfull boxes and zero undefined references. Fourteen test suites pass, including the new residue/repeated-mention tests; the freeze manifest contains 48 consistent entries. README, the English mirror, annotation handbook, declarations, both submission versions, highlights and the arXiv abstract are synchronized. No internal endpoints or keys are present in the tree.

**Remaining limitations (unchanged and disclosed):** no third-party corpus; holdout human utility scoring prepared-but-not-collected; no available local neural service and therefore no local-neural end-to-end claim; every neural condition is cloud-assisted and diagnostic only, with instance-level rather than full model/version/prompt intervals; unpinned model snapshots and provider-dependent seed honoring; literal-graded rather than paraphrase, multi-hop and public-knowledge attacks.

Code, synthetic data (including holdout-4), protocols v1–v6, deviation log D-R1–D-R34, raw outputs and analysis scripts are public at https://github.com/hurrywhen/dr-sl and https://gitee.com/jiangsu-yunhefeng-intelligent_0/dr-sl. Author-order, conflict-of-interest, funding, ethics and prior-version disclosures are in `declarations.md` and `../authorship_contribution_memo.md`; fields requiring author confirmation are explicitly marked. No acceptance status is claimed.
