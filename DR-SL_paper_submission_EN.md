## Title

Auditing Local Release Gates for Cloud-Local LLM Inference: Failure Modes of a Runtime-Sensitive-Set De-identification Chain

## Three core findings

1. **Missed extraction → false release.** Holdout-1 (shared vocabulary): 46/80 released; 36/46 (78.3%, Wilson [64.4,87.7]) retain gold literals; PASS gold L 0.147 > ALL 0.121. Banking 20/20 released with 20/20 residue ([83.9,100]); legal PASS 10/10 with residue ([72.2,100]). External FR_LLM|PASS 0.826 (19/23, [62.9,93.0]).
2. **Utility gate rewards unknown task coverage (perverse incentive).** Task-relevant recall 0.493 vs attribute recall 0.858; 37.5% harmful unmarked-context fallback (the scored context is extractor-unmarked, NOT certified non-sensitive); 30/46 releases (65%) via harmful fallback, ALL with gold-literal residue (FR 1.000 vs 0.375 other releases). Coverage gate (UNSCORABLE→human): h1 releases 46→5 (57.5%→6.2%); among the 5, no false release observed (0/5, Wilson [0,0.434])—too few to validate safety; h2 release 93.8%→37.5%, FR still 1.0 (partial coverage blind). A routing policy with real cost, not a certificate; schema-level coverage is future work.
3. **No closed-loop increment.** Paired release/gold-L deltas 0[0,0] on three holdouts; local-utility delta NEGATIVE on all three (h1 −0.089[−0.117,−0.061], h2 −0.017[−0.032,−0.003], h3 −0.101[−0.130,−0.071]; fixed h3 −0.116[−0.149,−0.084]); online GEN equal external attack-hit rate, higher judge utility (0.438 vs 0.362); downstream preservation paired Δ +0.025[0,0.0625] (primary judge online_alt, 2/40 disagree) / +0.025[−0.0375,0.0875] (robustness online_fast, 8/40 disagree), 23/40 identical artifacts — no detectable mean difference, not equivalence.

**Metric construct (round 6).** `FR_gold` is a gold-annotated task/sensitive literal-residue audit proxy, not demonstrated privacy damage or re-identification. Explicit identifiers, quasi-identifiers, and task-critical values with context-dependent stand-alone sensitivity are distinguished. `0元` is conservatively annotated under personal-financial and banking-task rules; this does not assert that a zero fee alone is private or identifying. The frozen small-fee generator's whole-thousand quantization yields zero values; the primary metric and frozen data are unchanged, with exclusion sensitivity reported separately.

## Supporting analyses (round 3/4/5/6)

- **Bugs vs architecture.** Fixed extractor (clear defects only: money pattern wiring, ASCII email local part, allergy label/collision; no lexicon additions; original frozen, hash-verified). Diagnostic h1: recall→1.000, no observed gold false release among 46 releases (0/46, Wilson [0,0.077]), decisions unchanged 80/80, over-extraction 0.300→0.188, remaining 13.8% fallback entirely benign (harmful 0). Diagnostic h2: recall 0.668, FR still 1.0. Confirmatory h3 (natural 50/50 mix, seed 20261122, template family #3, realized old fraction 0.389): FR 100%[92.0,100]→82.9%[68.7,91.5], recall 0.542→0.713, task recall 0.427→0.792, harmful fallback 30%→5%. Persistent false release after fixes = structural runtime-S blind spot. Contrast table reports all FR with Wilson intervals and fallback trichotomy (all/benign/harmful).
- **Neural extractor contrast (round 4; round-5 interpretation fix; diagnostic).** Cloud-assisted qwen3.8-max, frozen prompt (declared taxonomy, verbatim values, no gold/lexicons), 200/200 calls, 0 unlocatable; same gates on h1/h3/drift-p0: exact-span recall 0.965/0.973/0.965, task recall 1.000/0.971/1.000, FR 16.1%[7.1,32.6]/16.7%[6.7,35.9]/13.3%[3.7,37.9], harmful fallback 0; paired release-rate/gold-$L$ deltas 0, U deltas negative. Neural recall survives FULL vocabulary displacement (deterministic 0.522): the large drift failure is implementation-specific vocabulary dependence. FR source decomposition (round-5 P0): h1 5/31 and drift-p0 2/15 are ENTIRELY the undeclared room_no type (taxonomy-coverage gap—a pessimistic conditional outcome of the incomplete prompt); only h3 4/24 (all city; no proposal contains the gold city string, with one lexically related hotel name) directly evidences within-declared-taxonomy neural misses. The frozen taxonomy omitted THREE gold types (room_no/marriage_years/work_years; h3 fully declared; work_years values were proposed as duration and scrubbed); an automatic coverage test pins the disclosure. Income misses are PARTIAL-SPAN extraction (gold "monthly income about 12000 yuan" → verbatim substring "12000 yuan", locatable; NOT non-verbatim): gold-entity character coverage 0.983/0.988/0.983 exceeds exact-span recall; partial-span-only gold values 10/10/4 (all income). Bound scoping: exact-span recall is conservative under the current prompt/taxonomy; FR is conditional on the incomplete taxonomy with NO general bound direction (omitted types can raise FR). Trust disclosure: extraction itself discloses X → diagnostic only, not a deployable local gate; single-run, instance-level intervals only. Prompt NOT amended post hoc; the complete-taxonomy holdout-4 contrast is reinterpreted with direct extraction evidence in round 6.
- **Neural credibility experiments and round-6 mechanism corrections (round 5: 260 calls; round 6: archive-only, 0 calls; all diagnostic).** (1) Sampling stability: two frozen-prompt repeats on a deterministic 60-instance subset (seeds 20261126/20261127) give exact entity-set agreement 95.0%/91.7% vs round 4 (Jaccard 0.991/0.985), yet release and FR instance sets are IDENTICAL across all three qwen runs (FR 3/20 [5.2,36.0])—sampling perturbs spans, not the audited decisions here. (2) Second family: glm-5.2 (same prompt/subset, seed 20261128) matches 68.3% of entity sets (Jaccard 0.925) with similar recall (0.959); FR 5/25 comprises two room_no gaps and three zero-charge short-form residues. The round-5 phrase “never proposed” was wrong: GLM proposed and scrubbed `charge=0元手续费`, but the appended clarification's short `0元` remains. Symmetric five-way proposal relations and repeated-mention regression tests now pin this as `gold_substring_of_proposal` / span-variant residue. Excluding zero-charge residues changes only GLM FR (5/25→2/25, both gaps); all deterministic and other neural counts are unchanged. Miss/span identity is family-dependent; inheritance is similar, not identical. (3) Holdout-4: `FR_gap=0/32` under the complete taxonomy is a DEFINITIONAL IDENTITY, not safety evidence. Direct evidence is 10/10 exact type-and-value extraction and zero released residue for EACH of room_no, marriage_years and work_years. City values are 59/60 exact by value and 54/60 with a city label (five address); total FR is 1/32 [0.6,15.7], again the same county literal. Value/template omission and city/county/address type-boundary explanations remain unresolved; no causal blind-spot claim is made. h4 deterministic reference reproduces h3 (original FR 100% [93.0,100]; fixed 84.0% [71.5,91.7]). Round-4 results remain diagnostic under the original taxonomy; not recomputed with v2.

Drift series (h2 templates constant, overlap p∈{0,.25,.5,.75,1}, 40/level, seeds 20261123–27): realized overlap 0→1 gives recall 0.522→0.845, release 92.5%→57.5%, FR 100%→82.6% (monotonic).
